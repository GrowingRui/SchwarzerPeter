How to play

Match and discard as many pairs as you can, and don’t be the one left holding the Schwarzer Peter when the game ends.

The pairs
15 to 18 matching pairs, 30 to 36 cards depending on the deck.
The Schwarzer Peter
One single card with no partner. In our deck, it’s the black cat.
  1. 1

    Deal and sort

    The dealer shuffles and deals out every card. It’s normal for some players to get one more than others. Everyone lays any matching pairs face up on the table and keeps the rest hidden.

  2. 2

    Draw a card, clockwise

    The player to the dealer’s left starts by holding out their cards face down to the next player, who picks one at random. A match is laid down face up. Otherwise the card stays in their hand.

  3. 3

    Pass it on

    Whoever just drew now offers their own hidden cards to the next player. Hands shrink as pairs are laid down, and anyone who gets rid of all their cards is out and safe.

  4. 4

    The last one with the cat loses

    Play goes on until only the Schwarzer Peter is left. The player holding it is the only loser.
